Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: decorative background for SafeQR Japanese App Store screenshots. Create a premium, calm, friendly editorial 3D illustration background, portrait composition about 9:19.5. Deep midnight navy lower field, soft emerald and mint glass light flowing in a quiet arc along the right edge, small frosted translucent rounded tiles suggesting a readable QR scan outline in the bottom right, subtle satin grain and beautiful soft studio lighting. Most of the central 65 percent must remain a dark quiet empty area for an actual app screenshot to be placed later; top 25 percent is clean dark negative space for Japanese headlines. The decoration is purely abstract and must not imply that security is guaranteed. No letters, no words, no numbers, no logo, no phone, no app UI, no charts, no readable/scannable QR code, no locks, no shields, no neon cyberpunk. Refined Japanese utility-app marketing; muted colors, high visual clarity, no clutter.
